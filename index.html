How to play
Your goal: be the last one standing.

Every day (3½ minutes)

  • 1. Solve the maze at the lobby terminal. It also opens the garden. Skip it: −1 life.
  • 2. Earn coins: maze coin bags, the lobby games (block tennis and two arcade machines, one win a day each) and garden bushes (hold still 3 s).
  • 3. Sleep in your bed: in the last 30 s press 3 in your own bed. Not in bed when night falls (even in your room): −1 life.
  • You have 3 lives. 50 coins to start, +200 every morning.

Night

  • A bomb hurts whoever is inside that room at night: the owner, and any guest too. If you are not in your room, your room's bomb can't hurt you.
  • The morning report tells you exactly what happened.

The shop (two pockets)

  • Fake bomb 50 (does nothing, can't be disarmed) · Bomb 150 (visible, hold E 1 s to disarm) · Hidden bomb 300 (only a Defuser removes it).
  • Magnifier shows hidden bombs · Defuser 300 · Lock / Key · Trap · Slap · Silent steps · Extra life 500 · Shield (one hit, until morning).
  • The knife (400, one use) is sold only by the tunnel seller.

Bodies & votes

  • A stabbed player loses 1 life and lies on the floor for 15 s; the blood stays.
  • Find it and press E: everyone goes to the meeting room and votes. Pick a name or IDK. Most votes loses a life. The victim can't vote or talk.
  • With only 3 left there is no vote.

The house

  • Rooms have a front door and a back door into the dark tunnels. A locked door stops everyone, both ways — lock yourself in and you stay in until morning (or a Key opens it).
  • Your room has a chest only you can open. Closets let you hide. Footprints fade after 5 s. Every second day the lights go out for 30 s.
  • The camera room in the west tunnel shows every room on its own screen. While someone watches, the room cameras blink red.

Ghosts

Dead? You float anywhere, see everything (even hidden bombs) and chat only with ghosts. Hold E next to a living player to let only them see you while you hold it.
